Our paper *[Topology-Dependent Mechanical Failure of Knotted Polymer Chains from Reactive Molecular Dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.macromol.6c01298)* is published in **Macromolecules**. The first author is Zhirui Xiang, former undergraduate student and research assistant in our group and now a PhD student at the University of Notre Dame. The work is a collaboration with Tianhang Zhou (China University of Petroleum, Beijing) and Francesco Zonta (XJTLU).
